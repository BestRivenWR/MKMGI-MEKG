    MKMGI × MEKGCode & documentation ↗B73 v5
    Methods & data

    MKMGI combines a temporal Transformer, attention pooling and two residual relational GCN layers. Scores rank candidate genes; temporal weights describe model pooling.

    MEKG organizes evidence by source and experiment type. Materials, stages, reference assemblies and source links are retained in record details.

    Data use

    Questions, recent conversation, public sources and the current gene's model summary are sent to DeepSeek to generate answers. Raw omics and unpublished experimental files remain on the server.
